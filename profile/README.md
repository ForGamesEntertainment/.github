## UnFairFlips

**Our latest project · In development**

### One more flip. One unlikely tavern.

Pull up a chair at **Bear Paws Tavern**. There’s a coin on the table, a bear at the bar, and more to discover as you play.

**UnFairFlips** starts with a simple action: flip a coin. Earn money, choose your next upgrade, and open up more of the room around you. Between flips, meet the tavern’s characters and explore its little interactions.

| Make every flip count | Make yourself at home | Enjoy the company |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Invest in upgrades to chance, flip speed, combos, and coin value. | Unlock the journal, tip jar, radio, and the fox’s dice as you progress. | Share the room with a bear and a fox, discover dialogue, and build a bottle collection. |

**A small room with more going on than first meets the eye.** Collect tips, explore the tavern, and decide what to improve next.

UnFairFlips is still in development. Features and presentation may change; a release date has not been announced here.

**Follow [ForGames Entertainment](https://github.com/ForGamesEntertainment)** for future announcements and public project updates.
